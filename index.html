ABOUT_ME

Hi! I'm Taika, a second-year Software Development student. Alongside my studies, I have independently developed my skills in cybersecurity and offensive security.

I have around three years of self-taught experience exploring red team methodologies, penetration testing, vulnerability discovery, and understanding how systems work from an attacker's perspective.

In software development, I am particularly interested in how applications can be built efficiently, securely, and maintainably. Cybersecurity gives me another perspective on the same problem: understanding how systems can be broken, identifying their weaknesses, and figuring out how those weaknesses can be addressed.

Outside of my studies and cybersecurity, I also have a side interest in animation and creating visual experiences through code. I enjoy experimenting with JavaScript and WebGL to create interactive graphics, animations, and other visual projects.
